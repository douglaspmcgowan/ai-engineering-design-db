# Playbook: Explore Buildup

**Goal:** Build a curated collection of relevant projects for a literature review or research gap analysis. Start broad, narrow progressively, export.

---

## Step 1 — Anchor on a known paper

In Browse search, find 1–3 papers you already know well. Click each card to open the detail panel. Click **Save to collection** → create a new collection (e.g. `lit-review-2025`).

These anchors define your semantic center.

---

## Step 2 — Expand via Explorer's Find Similar

Click **Explorer** in the nav. Single-click one of your anchor projects to open its detail panel.  
Click **Find Similar** — the graph enters similarity focus mode and shows the nearest neighbors by technique overlap.

Inspect each neighbor. For relevant ones: right-click → **Copy ID**, then return to Browse and search by ID or name.  
Save the relevant ones to your collection.

---

## Step 3 — Technique-driven expansion

Back in Browse, filter by each technique used by your anchor projects (if technique filters exist) or search for specific technique names.

For each new result:
- Is the technique the same or a precursor?
- Is the year before or after your anchor?
- Is the application domain the same?

Add "yes to 2 of 3" papers to the collection.

---

## Step 4 — Organization sweep

In Browse, use the Organization filter to see all projects from the labs already in your collection.  
This catches related work from the same group that uses different terminology.

---

## Step 5 — Timeline sanity check

Switch to **Timeline** view with your collection active.  
A healthy literature review should have:
- At least 1–2 foundational papers (oldest year group)
- A "peak" cohort (when the field matured)
- 1–2 very recent papers (current state of the art)

If you're missing any tier, search for it explicitly.

---

## Step 6 — Open collection as graph

In the sidebar, click the 🕸 icon next to your collection name. This opens Explorer pre-filtered to your collection nodes.  
Look for isolated nodes (not connected to others in the collection) — these may be outliers to re-evaluate, or bridges to unexplored subfields.

---

## Step 7 — Export

In Browse with your collection filtered, switch to **Table** view. Select all → copy to clipboard for a spreadsheet, or screenshot for a slide.

Shareable URL: the active filters are encoded in the URL hash — paste it to share the exact filtered view with a collaborator.

---

## Output checklist

- [ ] 15–40 papers in collection (typical for a focused lit review)
- [ ] At least 3 organizations represented
- [ ] At least 2 year cohorts represented
- [ ] At least 2 distinct technique families represented
- [ ] 0 duplicate entries
- [ ] All paper URLs open to actual abstracts
