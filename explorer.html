What the graph shows

Nodes are AI/ML projects, organizations, categories, techniques, venues, and people. Edges show relationships — who built what (BUILT_BY), what it references (CITES/BUILT_ON), and how similar things are (SEMANTICALLY_NEAR). The Embed view uses UMAP to place nodes by semantic similarity — clusters are real neighborhoods in 1536-dim embedding space.

How to explore

• Click any node to see details in the right panel
• Double-click to enter Focus Mode — see only that node's neighborhood
• Embed view (◉) places nodes by semantic similarity, colored by cluster
• Sidebar lets you filter by node type, edge type, category, and year
